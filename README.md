# AGM Productions — Portfolio Website

A responsive photography and videography portfolio website for AGM Productions.

## Folder structure

AGM_Productions_Full_Website/
├── index.html
├── README.md
├── css/
│   └── style.css
├── js/
│   └── script.js
└── assets/
    ├── images/
    │   └── agm-logo.png
    └── videos/

## How to use

1. Open `index.html` in a browser to preview the website.
2. Replace the portfolio placeholders with your own images.
3. Add your video files or video embeds in the Video Portfolio section.
4. Open `js/script.js` and replace:
   `YOUR_EMAIL@example.com`
   with your business email.
5. The Facebook and Instagram links are already connected:
   https://www.facebook.com/AGMProduc
   https://www.instagram.com/agm.productions/

## Adding your photos

Place your images inside `assets/images/`, then update the portfolio cards in `index.html` with an `<img>` element.

Example:
<img src="assets/images/wedding-01.jpg" alt="Wedding photography">

## Publishing

You can upload the complete folder to most static hosting services or your web hosting's public HTML directory.
